MUSE Blinding

Thread model

We do NOT want to fight malicious actors. We do want:
  • Stop accidental physics releases
  • Blind ourselves to mitigate analyzer bias

Approach

  • Our code and data is accessible to everyone in the collaboration
  • Slowest analysis step: Tracking
  • Blinding applied to tracking output
Select tracks probabilistically and encrypt.
Selection function depends on:
  • Track angle
  • Particle Species
  • Data or MC
